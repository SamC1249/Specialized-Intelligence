# Adversarial-Agent Plan — 2026-07-17

> Prior plans read on this branch: `docs/plan-2026-06-20.md` (bootstrap).
> Baseline: `reports/baseline-2026-06-20.json` — mean quality per source
> on the seed fixtures: wikimedia 0.62 > peertube 0.57 >
> common_crawl 0.53 > archive_org 0.36; `__total__` 0.499.
>
> Parallel branches (unmerged) have proposed dedup (SimHash), license
> audit, C2PA-style payload hashing, procedural-density scoring, and
> small legally-clean source expansion. Today attacks a different
> frontier: **corpus adversarial robustness, legal signal enforcement,
> and world-model-shaped curation** — the axes not yet covered.

## Where we are, honestly

- Everything on `main` is still the 2026-06-20 seed pipeline: four
  adapters, one metadata-only scorer, one comparison harness, one
  baseline JSON. The baseline has not moved in ~4 weeks of coding-agent
  effort because the good branches keep landing in parallel and never
  merging.
- We have zero defenses against the biggest 2026 web-data risk:
  **AI-generated recipe pages now dominate the English long tail**
  (Graphite estimates ≥ 50% of English web content since May 2025 is
  LLM-authored) and our text-density heuristic *rewards* long
  auto-generated descriptions.
- We have zero enforcement of the new formal opt-out signals
  (`Content-Usage: train-ai=n` in HTTP headers and `robots.txt`, per
  IETF `draft-ietf-aipref-attach-04` / `-vocab-04`). Common Crawl
  captures the header field but we never parse it. If a page owner
  opted their content out of AI training, we would happily ingest it
  and mislabel the record as license-clean.
- The V-JEPA 2 team's most-cited data lesson (+1.4 points on average
  from cluster-based retrieval against a *target distribution* of
  Kinetics / SSv2 / COIN / EPIC-KITCHENS) is not reflected anywhere in
  our scorer. We rank by uniform component weights, not by proximity
  to the world-model target distribution.
- We treat `License.CC_BY_NC*` as `RESTRICTED`, which means the two
  *highest-density procedural cooking corpora on the planet* —
  EPIC-KITCHENS-100, Ego4D, Ego-Exo4D, EPFL-Smart-Kitchen-30 — cannot
  even enter the pipeline for *research-only* pretraining ablations.
  That is a documentation bug pretending to be a safety property.
- Prelinger / NARA / USDA (`A Step-Saving Kitchen`, hundreds of
  ephemeral food-preparation films) are unambiguously U.S. public
  domain and *not indexed by any of our four adapters*.

## Hypotheses under test today

> **H6 — AI-slop dominates the license-clean text-heavy tail.** For
> Common Crawl recipe pages published after 2024-01-01, an "AI-slop
> score" computed from metadata alone (author absent, `datePublished`
> after 2024, boilerplate n-grams like `"Once you've done that,
> simply enjoy"`, missing `dateModified`, host TLD ∈ new-gTLD set,
> `recipe_steps` length in an LLM-typical band) predicts human
> "would not train on this" ratings with ≥ AUC 0.75 on a small
> hand-graded fixture. If true, we must add it as a negative weight in
> the scorer; if false, we've falsified a widespread intuition and
> should stop pretending metadata detection can save us.

> **H7 — Formal opt-out signals are already the binding legal
> constraint.** ≥ 5 % of Common Crawl HTML captures already carry
> `Content-Usage: train-ai=n` (or the `robots.txt Content-Usage` rule)
> as of 2026-Q2, so ignoring them is not a hypothetical risk — it is
> present-tense unlicensed training. Any record whose page emits
> `train-ai=n` must be reclassified to `RESTRICTED` before scoring.

> **H8 — Cluster-retrieval curation, even with metadata-only
> embeddings, beats uniform-weight scoring on `mean_quality_unique`.**
> Following V-JEPA 2 §10.2 and Oquab et al. 2024 (hierarchical
> k-means), a TF-IDF centroid over a small target-distribution seed
> (HowTo100M / COIN / EPIC-KITCHENS title bags) and a nearest-neighbor
> re-ranker over our candidate pool should Pareto-dominate the current
> WEIGHTS-sum on any reasonable held-out fixture. This is testable
> today with zero GPUs and zero downloads.

> **H9 — CC-BY-NC egocentric video is the highest-signal training
> substrate we are legally allowed to touch for research.** Collapsing
> it to `RESTRICTED` (as we do today) throws away every hour of
> EPIC-KITCHENS / Ego4D / Ego-Exo4D / EPFL-Smart-Kitchen-30. The
> correct fix is a `NC_RESEARCH_ONLY` license tier that flows through
> a *use-case-aware* compare harness (`--use-case research` vs
> `--use-case commercial`), not a single `is_redistributable` bit.

> **H10 — US-government / Prelinger PD film is unexploited yield.**
> The National Archives Catalog + `collection:prelinger` on Internet
> Archive should each contribute *at least* an additional +10 hours of
> license-clean cooking / food-prep footage per crawl. Since IA is
> already in our allowlist, `sources/prelinger_on_ia.py` is a
> constant-factor cost; NARA is a new-source cost. Both are strictly
> additive to the baseline.

## Adversarial questions to attack

1. **Do our tests actually enforce the guarantees we claim?**
   - We claim tests are offline: nothing greps `tests/` for
     `httpx.Client(` / `urllib.request.urlopen` / `requests.get`.
     Add a test that fails if any test file uses live networking APIs.
   - We claim on-disk reports match `BenchmarkResult` forever:
     nothing loads `reports/*.json` back through the Pydantic model.
     Add a schema-contract test.
   - We claim the baseline is a ratchet: nothing re-runs the harness
     against the fixtures and compares to `baseline-2026-06-20.json`.
     Add a regression test with a documented tolerance.
   - We claim daily planning happens: `main` has *one* plan from
     2026-06-20. Add a plan-freshness check that fails CI when
     `SPECINT_ENFORCE_PLAN_FRESHNESS=1` and the newest
     `docs/plan-*.md` is > 30 days stale. Default to *warn* so
     unmerged coding branches aren't forced to bump the plan.
2. **Can we detect AI-slop from metadata alone?**
   - Build `quality/ai_slop.py` with a rule-based scorer over the
     signals listed in H6. Return a value in [0, 1] where 1 = "smells
     like slop." Seed 10-record hand-graded fixture; assert
     rank-correlation ≥ 0.7.
   - Add `n_ai_slop_flagged` to the additive `license_audit` payload
     in the JSON report (do **not** break `BenchmarkResult` schema).
3. **Can we enforce IETF AIPREF opt-outs today?**
   - `quality/aipref.py` — parse `Content-Usage` header dict values
     and `robots.txt Content-Usage` rules per the current drafts.
     Provide `has_train_ai_optout(headers: dict, robots: str) -> bool`.
   - Wire it into `sources/common_crawl.py`: when `train-ai=n` is
     present, force `license=License.RESTRICTED` regardless of the
     page's stated CC license. Log the demotion in `provenance.query`.
4. **Can we imitate cluster-retrieval curation without a GPU?**
   - `quality/target_distribution.py` — build a TF-IDF centroid over
     a checked-in seed of ~200 target-distribution titles (COIN and
     EPIC-KITCHENS taxonomies are open text). Rank candidates by
     cosine similarity. Emit `n_on_target` and
     `mean_similarity_on_target` in the additive payload. Optional
     new scorer `v3-target` in the (future) scorer registry.
5. **Can we responsibly ingest NC-licensed egocentric data?**
   - Extend `License` with `CC_BY_NC`, `CC_BY_NC_SA`, `CC_BY_ND`,
     `CC_BY_NC_ND` values *plus* a class method
     `permits(use_case: str) -> bool` that returns True for `research`
     but False for `commercial` on all NC/ND tiers. Update
     `db_structured.md`. Add a use-case flag to the CLI compare.
6. **Which underused legal sources should we ship today?**
   - `sources/nara.py` — National Archives Catalog `/api/v2/records`
     JSON adapter; fixture-only search on the Bureau of Human
     Nutrition and Home Economics record group. All records default
     to `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` because 44 U.S.C. §105 forbids federal
     copyright.
   - `sources/prelinger_on_ia.py` — thin `ArchiveOrgSource` subclass
     that pins `collection:prelinger` and defaults the license to
     `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` when the item's `licenseurl` is empty (Prelinger
     donates under PD by default).

## Risks the next reviewer should challenge

- **The AI-slop scorer will over-fire on legitimate cooking-blog SEO
  boilerplate** ("First, you'll want to preheat your oven to
  350 °F..."). Mitigation: publish precision/recall on the hand-graded
  fixture and require Coding-Agent to keep both above 0.6.
- **AIPREF is still a draft.** `draft-ietf-aipref-attach` is expired
  (revision -04 lapsed May 2026 under the 6-month rule) though the WG
  milestone is Aug 2026. If the syntax changes we will need to update
  the parser. Isolate it in `quality/aipref.py` so downstream code
  doesn't hard-code the format.
- **Target-distribution curation can *narrow* the corpus toward
  concepts we already know about.** V-JEPA 2.1's own retraction of
  this idea (they *reduced* Kinetics weight and *3.8×'ed* YT-1B in
  the follow-up) is worth citing in the artifact — do not oversell
  cluster retrieval as monotonic.
- **`NC_RESEARCH_ONLY` license tier is an escape hatch for exactly
  the mistake we're trying to avoid.** Document loud, red-flag output
  every time a `--use-case research` compare is run, and add a
  regression test that a `--use-case commercial` compare would drop
  those hours to zero.
- **Plan-freshness enforcement will make old branches unmergeable if
  we hard-fail on CI.** Keep it env-gated on `main` only.

## Metrics we will report in tomorrow's
`reports/compare-2026-07-18.json`

Per source, plus `__total__`, keeping the existing schema and adding
an additive `audit` payload:

- Baseline columns: `n_records`, `n_license_clean`, `total_duration_s`,
  `mean_quality`, `p50_quality`, `p90_quality`, `unique_authors`.
- Audit map (new, additive; NOT in `BenchmarkResult`):
  - `n_unknown_fallthrough` — records whose license fell through to
    `UNKNOWN`.
  - `n_ai_slop_flagged` — records with `ai_slop_score >= 0.6`.
  - `n_train_ai_optout` — records demoted by AIPREF enforcement.
  - `mean_similarity_on_target` — cosine mean of the target
    distribution ranker.
  - `n_use_case_research_only` — records that would be dropped by
    `--use-case commercial` (i.e., NC/ND tiers).

   the enforcement actually fires when it should).
3. AI-slop scorer rank correlation ≥ 0.7 vs the hand-graded fixture.
4. NARA + Prelinger sources contribute at least 4 additional
   `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` records on the seed query.
