# Artifact — Pro2Bench (Plan, Watch, Recover)

**Date filed:** 2026-08-12
**Filed by:** Adversarial-Agent
**Primary URL:** <https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04970v1>
**Cited in plan:** `docs/plan-2026-08-12.md`

## One-sentence summary

Pro2Bench re-annotates five existing procedural-video corpora (Ego4D,
Ego-Exo4D, EPIC-KITCHENS, HoloAssist, HowTo100M) plus a new
`EgoProactive` set into a **single unified schema of "golden plan +
per-decision-point in-plan/out-of-plan/recovery labels"** — 42,275
eval instances, 249,584 training instances, 14 domains. It's the
current best target schema for procedural-assistance world models.

## Why it matters to us

- Our current output is *records*. Pro2Bench's output is *decision
  points*. Between them sits a schema translation we should design
  early, even though we don't need to ship it yet.
- The `Recipe.recipeInstructions` list on the web is a natural fit
  for Pro2Bench's "golden plan" abstraction — a sequence of steps
  the learner must recover if the observed trajectory deviates.
- Their `Out-of-Plan (OOP)` label is 3,433 instances *only* in the
  new EgoProactive corpus, because the older ones don't carry that
  signal. If we can *synthesize* OOP signals from web recipe
  variants ("if you don't have X, substitute Y") we have a novel
  angle.

## Ideation — how it changes what we ship

1. **Schema-forward design.** When we finalise a
   `RecipeStepRecord` (v2) it should carry (a) `step_index`, (b)
   `verb_lemma`, (c) `ingredient_refs`, (d) optional
   `substitute_of` and `precondition`. This is the minimal schema
   that maps cleanly onto Pro2Bench decision points.
2. **Recipe-variation signal.** Wikibooks Cookbook recipes and
   permissive food-blog archives often say "if you don't have X,
   substitute Y" — this is *free* OOP supervision. Add a
   `substitutions_detected` count to future benchmark rows.
3. **Cross-domain generalization framing.** Pro2Bench spans 14
   domains. That validates the AGENTS.md claim that this repo must
   generalize to surgery, lab work, sports, manufacturing — the
   *procedural* pattern is domain-agnostic.

## Risks

- Pro2Bench is not itself downloadable video data; it's an
  annotation layer on top of research corpora. Don't treat it as a
  source. Treat it as a target schema.
- Their unified schema is opinionated and could shift as the field
  matures. Keep our `VideoRecord` upstream of any target schema; do
  translation at consumption time.
