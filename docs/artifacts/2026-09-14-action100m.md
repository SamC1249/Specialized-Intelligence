# ACTION100M — Fully-automated procedural-video annotation at scale

*CVPR 2026 EgoVis Workshop; Chen et al., Meta AI.*

## What it does

- Starts from **1.2M internet instructional videos** (~14.6 years of
  runtime) and produces **~100M temporally-localized action segments**
  with open-vocabulary supervision.
- Pipeline is fully automated:
  1. **Hierarchical temporal segmentation** using **V-JEPA 2**
     embeddings — no shot-boundary heuristics, purely learned.
  2. **Tree-of-Captions** at multiple granularities (frame → clip →
     segment) so downstream models can align at whatever temporal
     scale they need.
  3. **Self-refine loop** with a reasoning model (GPT-OSS-120B) that
     aggregates evidence across sibling captions.
- Downstream: trains a VL-JEPA model that shows monotonic data-scaling
  and strong zero-shot action recognition.

## Why it matters for us

- This is *exactly* the pipeline shape we want to build — but the
  authors seeded it from HowTo100M-style YouTube URLs (all-rights-
  reserved). That is the trap AGENTS.md hard-constraint #1 exists to
  prevent.
- Everything from segmentation onward is license-agnostic: if we can
  supply the same volume of *legally-permissive* input clips, we can
  re-use their playbook wholesale.
- V-JEPA 2 embeddings are open (Facebook Research repo,
  `arxiv/2506.09985`). Adopting them for segmentation lets us skip
  the shot-detection detour that Panda-70M spends most of its
  compute on.

## How we'd use it here

- **Plan hook:** motivates H2/H5 in `docs/plan-2026-09-14.md` — cheap
  metadata quality signals are only Phase-0; the real leverage is
  a V-JEPA-2-driven segmentation stage once we have hours of
  legally-clean video.
- **Concrete follow-up (future PR):** a
  `src/specint/segment/vjepa2.py` that takes a `VideoRecord` with
  `media_url` and yields `Segment(record_id, start_s, end_s,
  embedding)`. Fixture-tested with a canned 3-frame embedding tensor
  (no model weights checked in).
- **Guardrail:** any "action-100M-lite" corpus we produce must reject
  any input `VideoRecord` whose `license.is_redistributable` is False.
  Enforceable today via the existing `License` enum.
