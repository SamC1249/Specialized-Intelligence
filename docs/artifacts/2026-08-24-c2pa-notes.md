- **Spec (v2.4):** <https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/C2PA_Specification.html>
- **Content Credentials spec:** <https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/ContentCredentials.html>
- **AI/ML use-case white paper (2026-07):**
  <https://c2pa.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2026/07/Use-of-Content-Credentials-to-Identify-Synthetic-and-Non-Synthetic-Content.pdf>
- **Adoption (Jan 2026):** 6,000+ member/affiliate organizations
  including Google, Meta, OpenAI, Sony, Nikon, Leica.

## What C2PA gives us

A cryptographically signed *manifest* attached to an asset (image, video,
audio) that records:

- **Hard bindings** — hash of the asset bits (tamper-evident: any
  bit-flip invalidates the claim signature).
- **Soft bindings** — perceptual fingerprints for identifying
  derivatives / renditions.
- **Assertions**, notably for us:
  - `c2pa.hash.collection.data` (spec §9.2.5) — hash of a training
    dataset partition, letting a model prove which data it did or did
    not see.
  - `c2pa.actions.v2` with `inputTo` (spec §18.16.1) — declares an
    asset was fed into an AI inference or training run.
  - `Do Not Train` — a machine-readable rights reservation the owner
    can embed at creation time; a compliant crawler must honor it.

## What we can adopt *today* (no crypto required)

Ship a "C2PA-shaped" provenance record without X.509 or claim signatures:

- `Provenance.raw_sha256` — SHA-256 of the *raw upstream payload* we
  parsed (the JSON blob, HTML page, or WARC record). This is our
  analogue of a hard binding on the metadata side.
- `Provenance.record_jcs_sha256` — SHA-256 of the JCS-canonical
  (RFC 8785) serialization of the final `VideoRecord`. This is a
  stable identity across re-encodings of the same record.
- `Provenance.extractor_git` — the real short git SHA at extraction
  time (currently `"dev"`).
- `Provenance.rights` — the resolved rights signal (`robots.txt`,
  TDMRep, `ai.txt`, and any C2PA `Do Not Train` bit if the source
  ships one).

Once we start actually downloading video bytes, upgrade to a real
Manifest emitter (`c2pa` Rust crate has Python bindings) and start
signing with a test cert; publish the trust list.

## Anti-goal

Do **not** invent our own "provenance" scheme when C2PA already exists.
Our data structures should be *convertible* to C2PA manifest fields
without loss so a future signer can wrap them without a schema rewrite.
