# HowTo100M (Miech et al., ICCV 2019)

- Paper page: https://www.di.ens.fr/willow/research/howto100m/
- Code (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/antoine77340/HowTo100M

## Claim

136M narrated clips from 1.2M YouTube videos, across 23k tasks (cooking,
gardening, crafts, personal care). Aligns automatic YouTube captions with
short video segments — arguably the canonical instructional-video corpus.

## Legality under our constraints

**Out of allowlist.** The *code* is Apache-2.0 but the *content* is
YouTube-hosted, so any redistribution (or bulk download) violates
YouTube ToS. HuggingFace's `HuggingFaceM4/howto100m` card explicitly
lists licensing as "Not specified". This matches our `RESTRICTED`
policy: we may *reference* URLs for provenance, but must never store
frames or media_urls.

## Ideas we adopt

1. **Narration-conditioned filtering.** HowTo100M's ranking heavily
   uses caption-text density. Our metadata-only `text_density` scorer
   is a weak proxy — expand it via a new `procedural_density`
   component that also counts imperative verbs and time/temperature
   tokens (see plan-2026-07-18 §A2).
2. **Task taxonomy.** Their 23k activities are grouped into ~12 super
   categories. When we generalize past cooking, our `SourceQuery`
   should accept a super-category slug and expand it into seed terms
   deterministically.

## Not-adopted (yet)

- ASR-alignment pipeline: requires media download, which is outside our
  metadata-only mandate today.
