# V-JEPA and V-JEPA 2 — feature-prediction video pretraining

- **V-JEPA** — Bardes, Garrido, Ponce, Chen, Rabbat, LeCun, Assran, Ballas.
  ICLR 2024. [arXiv:2404.08471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08471) ·
  [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/jepa).
- **V-JEPA 2** — arXiv 2506.09985 (2025).
  [html](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09985).

## What they built

V-JEPA pretrains on **VideoMix2M**: ~2M videos assembled from
Kinetics-400/600/700 + HowTo100M + Something-Something-v2. The training
signal is entirely **feature-prediction with masking** — no language,
no negatives, no pixel reconstruction. Result: ViT-H/16 hits 81.9% on
Kinetics-400 with a frozen backbone.

V-JEPA 2 scales to **VideoMix22M** (~22M videos including YT1B, ~1.4M
video-hours) and — critically for us — does **retrieval-based curation
of YT1B against a target distribution** built from Kinetics +
Something-Something v2 + COIN + EpicKitchen, following the DINOv2
image-curation trick.

## What we can steal

- **Uncurated ≠ useful.** V-JEPA 2 explicitly finds that uncurated
  YT1B *hurts* performance until they cluster-retrieve against a
  balanced target distribution. This is the strongest published
  argument for our comparison-first rule: any new source that raises
  raw hours but drops mean quality is not obviously a win, and must be
  measured, not asserted.
- **Small clips are enough.** V-JEPA uses **16-frame clips at
  frame-skip 4 (~3 s)** for pretraining. That means the "60-minute
  cooking documentary" and the "3-minute how-to short" are equally
  useful pretraining substrate once we splice them into 3 s clips.
  Our current quality scorer over-rewards ~5-minute duration — we
  should soften that penalty to acknowledge that clip-level supervision
  makes duration less discriminating.
- **Masking strategy matters more than data volume past some point.**
  Anything that lets us claim "our corpus has diverse *motion*, not
  just diverse *scenes*" is worth its file size. Metric idea:
  compute average frame-difference magnitude on a strided sample of
  frames (out-of-scope until we start downloading) — reserve
  `motion_score` in the schema now.

## What we must *not* copy

- VideoMix2M's constituent datasets (Kinetics, HowTo100M, SSv2) are
  all research-only-license, and mostly ultimately point at YouTube.
  We reference the *training recipe*; we do not train on those
  corpora.
- Retrieval-based curation with a target distribution built from
  private datasets we don't have access to is a dead end for us. Our
  target distribution has to be assembled from license-clean anchors
  (e.g. Ego4D-style scenario labels re-derived from wikiHow taxonomy).

## Implementation hooks

- `src/specint/quality/metrics.py` → soften `_score_duration` (long
  videos still splice into good 3 s clips) and reserve a
  `motion_score` field.
- `src/specint/records.py` → add optional `motion_score: float | None`
  (default None). Present but ungated until we start extracting
  frames.
- `docs/plan-YYYY-MM-DD.md` → propose a "target distribution" file
  under `src/specint/curation/target.yaml`, populated from the
  wikiHow cooking taxonomy, that our future cluster-sampler reads.
