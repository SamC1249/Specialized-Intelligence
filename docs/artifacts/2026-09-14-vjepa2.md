# V-JEPA 2 — Self-supervised video world model

*arXiv 2506.09985 (2026); Meta AI FAIR.*

## What it does

- Two-stage self-supervised training on **>1M hours of internet video**
  (action-free JEPA pre-training) → optional **action-conditioned**
  post-training on <62 h of Droid robot trajectories.
- SOTA on physical-world benchmarks that matter to us:
  - **EPIC-Kitchens-100** action anticipation: **39.7 R@5**
    (previous best 27.6, PlausiVL). EK-100 is a cooking-heavy
    egocentric benchmark, so cooking video *is* the frontier
    evaluation for world models — this is direct evidence our
    domain choice is aligned with the field.
  - Something-Something v2: 77.3 top-1 (previous best 69.7).
  - PerceptionTest 84.0, TempCompass 76.9 (after LLM alignment).
- Zero-shot pick-and-place on a Franka arm with no in-lab data —
  the "world-model → planner" leap the field has been trying to
  make since 2022.

## Why it matters for us

- Meta explicitly frames V-JEPA 2 as "internet-scale video →
  world model", which is *our exact charter*. The paper's data
  curation section is a good adversarial mirror for our pipeline:
  they don't publish their curation methodology in detail, and we
  should treat that gap as an opportunity to be *more*
  reproducible.
- Their downstream evaluations (EK-100) confirm cooking as the
  right narrow target and give us a concrete metric to eventually
  race against: `mean_quality`-weighted training on our corpus
  should preserve or improve EK-100 R@5 relative to a same-hours
  YouTube-seeded baseline.
- V-JEPA 2 embeddings are the shared substrate ACTION100M
  (`./2026-09-14-action100m.md`) already builds on — reusing them
  keeps us composable with the emerging open ecosystem instead of
  inventing yet another encoder.

## How we'd use it here

- **Plan hook:** informs H2 (metadata is Phase 0). Longer term the
  Phase-1 quality scorer should include a V-JEPA-2 embedding norm
  or clip-embedding entropy computed from the free thumbnails
  every source publishes (`3` frames per record via `HEAD`, not a
  full download).
- **Concrete follow-up (future PR):** a `src/specint/quality/
  vjepa_probe.py` that computes an embedding-based novelty score
  from thumbnail(s) alone. Fixture-tested with a stub encoder that
  returns deterministic vectors.
- **Evaluation contract (future `compare` mode):** train a small
  linear probe on top of frozen V-JEPA 2 for EK-100 verb
  classification using each source's records; report probe
  accuracy in `BenchmarkResult.notes`. That is our first
  end-to-end downstream signal that a source is actually useful,
  not just legally clean.
