## Hypotheses under test

- **H2 (multilingual yield).** English-only seed terms miss the majority of
  cooking footage on Wikimedia Commons / PeerTube / Internet Archive.
  Adding a curated per-language seed list (es, fr, ja, zh, hi, ar, it, de,
  pt) will materially raise recall on the same fixtures once we simulate
  non-English titles.
- **H3 (procedural-density signal is metadata-usable).** A cheap
  metadata-only "language confidence" signal — proportion of ASCII vs CJK
  characters, presence of stop-word tokens, verb-density proxy — can
  discriminate low-effort clickbait titles from procedural cooking titles.
- **H4 (cross-source duplication is common).** The same Prelinger /
  Wikimedia / IA video appears in multiple registries. A dedup step keyed
  on (normalized_title, duration_bucket) will reveal that our "8-record
  fixture universe" contains at least one cross-source overlap, and the
  same effect will scale on the live web.
- **H5 (weights are asserted, not measured).** Today's quality weight
  vector (license 0.35, resolution 0.20, ...) is a guess. We need a
  systematic ablation harness so future weight PRs can prove they beat the
  baseline mean-quality on a held-out fixture set.

## Adversarial questions to attack today

1. Can we introduce a new signal (language confidence) *without* touching
   `WEIGHTS` in the same PR, so we can A/B compare the new signal in
   isolation? (Answer must be yes — the ablation harness demands it.)
2. Are we double-counting the same cooking video across `wikimedia` and
   `archive_org`? Prelinger content is often mirrored. Prove it or refute
   it on the current fixtures.
3. If Adversarial-Agent runs live-search once with SPECINT_RUN_INTEGRATION,
   are results deterministic *enough* to be committed as fixtures? If not,
   fixtures are the only unit of comparison and we must make them richer.

## Risks the next reviewer should challenge

- Language detection based on character ranges + stop words is coarse and
  can misfire on transliterated content (e.g. "sushi" in an English
  title). Mitigation: report `language_confidence` as a *hint*, do not
  filter records solely on it.
- Dedup on (title, duration) will incorrectly merge two distinct short
  clips ("pancakes.mp4", 180s). Bucket size and title normalization must
  be conservative; keep the *union* of provenance URLs on collapse so we
  can audit.
- Ablation harness must not fork randomness — every alternate weight
  vector must see the exact same input records; else "wins" are noise.
